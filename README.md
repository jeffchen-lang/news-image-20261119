A dense one-page infographic summing up Thursday 19 November 2026's world news — 25 stories in all. The lead is the GTA VI worldwide launch (Metacritic 99, $1.5B+ in pre-orders, 13 years in the making), followed by three columns: Games (Take-Two's jump, the Rockstar union vote, the day off to play), Markets & Economy (Nasdaq +0.4%, UNH −10.7%, NVDA −2.9%, hawkish FOMC minutes), and World & Tech (Australia jobless 4.9%, the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan, California's deluge, the Anthropic IPO). `main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte, every run.
